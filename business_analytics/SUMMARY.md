# Business Analytics Package - Summary

## Package Purpose
Business-focused analytics toolkit for measuring financial impact, ROI, and business value of experiments and product changes.

## What It Does
- **Financial Impact Analysis**: Calculate revenue impact, ROI, and profitability of experiments
- **Customer Value Modeling**: Integrate LTV, segment profitability, and customer economics
- **Data Quality Assurance**: Validate and clean business data for reliable analysis
- **Business Reporting**: Generate executive-ready summaries and visualizations
- **Cost-Benefit Analysis**: Evaluate experiment costs against business benefits

## Key Use Cases
1. **Experiment ROI**: Quantifying financial return on A/B tests and product changes
2. **Customer Segmentation**: Analyzing profitability across different customer segments
3. **Business Case Development**: Building financial justification for product investments
4. **Revenue Attribution**: Understanding which features/changes drive revenue
5. **Executive Reporting**: Creating business-focused summaries for leadership

## Target Users
- Business analysts calculating experiment ROI
- Product managers building business cases
- Finance teams evaluating product investments
- Executives reviewing experiment impact
- Data analysts focused on business metrics

## Business Focus
- **Revenue-Centric**: All analysis tied to business outcomes
- **Profitability Focus**: Understanding profit impact, not just revenue
- **Segment Economics**: Breaking down financial impact by customer segments
- **Cost Integration**: Including implementation and opportunity costs
- **Executive Communication**: Business-friendly visualizations and summaries

## Integration
- **Financial Systems**: Designed to integrate with revenue and cost data
- **Customer Data**: Links experiments to customer lifetime value
- **Reporting Tools**: Outputs compatible with business intelligence platforms
